How to Choose: Understanding the Two Types of "External"

Before spending any money, you must understand that not all external drives work the same way on a PS5.

Type 1: USB External Storage (Front Port)

This is what most people mean when they say "external SSD." You plug this into the USB-A or USB-C port on the front of the console.

  • Can you play PS5 games from it? No. You can only store PS5 games here. To play them, you must move them back to the internal SSD.
  • Can you play PS4 games from it? Yes. You can play PS4 games directly from a USB drive.
  • Best use case: Storing a massive library of PS4 games, backing up your save data, or storing media files.

Type 2: Internal M.2 NVMe SSD (Expansion Slot)

This is an SSD that goes inside the console. Technically, it is an "expansion SSD," but many users call it external because it is not part of the original factory storage.

  • Can you play PS5 games from it? Yes.
  • Can you play PS4 games from it? Yes.
  • Best use case: If you want to play PS5 games without constantly moving files back and forth, this is the only option.

Important Note: If your search for "external ssd for ps5" is driven by the need to play PS5 games without moving them to the main drive, you need to buy an Internal M.2 NVMe SSD, not a USB drive. If you just need space to store games you don't play often, a USB SSD is cheaper and easier to install.

Look for a portable SSD with a sleek, compact design. These drives usually have metal casings that help dissipate heat, which is crucial during long transfer sessions. Avoid cheap, unbranded drives found on marketplace sites; stick to reputable manufacturers that offer good warranty support.

Do You Actually Need an Internal M.2 SSD?

If you are primarily a PS5 gamer and find yourself constantly moving games back and forth to free up space, a USB drive will become frustrating. In this case, you should look at an Internal M.2 NVMe SSD.

This is not a USB drive. It is a stick of memory that you install inside the PS5. It allows you to play PS5 games directly from the expansion slot.

Key Requirements for Internal PS5 SSDs:

  1. Form Factor: M.2 2280 (most common) or 22110.
  2. Interface: PCIe Gen 4 NVMe.
  3. Speed: Minimum sequential read speed of 5,500 MB/s.
  4. Heatsink: Sony recommends an SSD with a heatsink. Some drives come with one; others require you to buy a separate heatsink.

Do not try to use a USB SSD as a replacement for an internal SSD. They serve different purposes. If you want seamless gameplay without moving files, this is the path you should take.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. Buying a USB Drive to Play PS5 Games

This is the most common error. You cannot play PS5 games directly from a USB external drive. You can only store them. If you buy a $100 USB SSD expecting to play your new PS5 titles from it, you will be disappointed. You must move the game to the internal storage first.

2. Ignoring the Heatsink Requirement (For Internal SSDs)

If you choose the internal route, many premium NVMe SSDs run hot. If you do not use a heatsink, the console may throttle the SSD's performance or, in extreme cases, detect an error. Check if the specific drive you buy requires a separate heatsink purchase or if it comes with one included.

3. Using Cheap, Unverified Brands

While you can find very cheap SSDs online, avoid unknown brands. Storage drives fail, and if your drive dies, you lose your data. Reputable brands offer warranties and better quality control. For a device as important as your game library, it is worth paying a premium for reliability.

4. Forgetting to Format the Drive

When you first plug in a new USB SSD or install an internal SSD, the PS5 will ask if you want to format it. You must select "USB Extended Storage" for USB drives. For internal drives, the console will handle the formatting process automatically during setup. Do not try to format the drive on a PC first; let the PS5 do it to ensure the correct file system is applied.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use an external SSD to play PS5 games?

No. You can only store PS5 games on an external USB SSD. To play them, you must move them to the internal SSD. You can, however, play PS4 games directly from an external USB SSD.

What is the fastest external SSD for PS5?

The speed of the external drive matters less than the USB port speed. A USB 3.2 Gen 2 drive (10Gbps) is the practical maximum for the PS5's front USB port. Any faster drive will not provide a significant real-world benefit for gameplay.

Do I need a heatsink for an internal PS5 SSD?

Yes, Sony strongly recommends using an SSD with a heatsink. If the SSD you choose does not come with one, you should purchase a compatible heatsink separately. This prevents overheating and ensures consistent performance.

Can I use an old external hard drive (HDD) for PS5?

Yes, but it is not recommended for gaming. It will work for storing PS4 games and media, but loading times will be slow. For a better experience, stick to SSDs.

How much storage do I need?

It depends on your habits. If you play many PS5 games, consider an internal SSD expansion of at least 2TB. If you mostly play PS4 games or want to store a large library, a 4TB USB SSD is a good starting point.

Final Thoughts

Choosing the right external SSD for your PS5 comes down to one simple question: Do you need to play PS5 games from this drive?

If the answer is yes, you need an Internal M.2 NVMe SSD. It is more complex to install but provides the seamless experience you are looking for.

If the answer is no, and you just need extra space for PS4 games, backups, or media, a USB External SSD is the perfect solution. Look for a reliable brand with a USB 3.0 or 3.2 connection. Avoid overpaying for extreme speeds that the console cannot fully utilize, and focus on capacity and reliability.
